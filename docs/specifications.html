PRISM HYBRID RECORDING CONSOLE

Specifications

A technical overview of the PRISM hybrid analog and digital recording console.

System Overview

PRISM is a modular professional recording console designed to combine the sonic characteristics of analog processing with modern digital control, recall, automation, and DAW integration.

Architecture Hybrid Analog / Digital

Analog audio processing combined with digitally controlled routing, recall, and automation.

Channel Count Up to 48 Inputs

Designed around a scalable multi-channel architecture.

Processing Modular

Channel processing is divided into replaceable, configurable modules.

Control Digital Recall

Analog processing parameters can be digitally monitored and recalled.
