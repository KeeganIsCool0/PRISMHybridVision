Help Build PRISM

PRISM Hybrid Vision is an independently developed hybrid analog/digital recording console designed to combine the character of analog audio with the flexibility of modern digital production.

Sponsorship helps turn the engineering work behind PRISM into physical hardware through prototyping, manufacturing, testing, and continued development.

Where Support Goes

PRISM is being developed as a long-term engineering project. Funding is used to move the project from schematics and simulations toward functional hardware and eventually a complete working console.

Engineering

Electronic components, PCB fabrication, development hardware, and engineering tools.

Prototyping

Channel strips, mix buses, control systems, modules, and prototype assemblies.

Manufacturing

Mechanical fabrication, enclosure components, connectors, wiring, and assembly.

Testing

Measurement equipment, validation, audio testing, and prototype verification.
