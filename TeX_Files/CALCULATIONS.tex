	\section*{Preamp Input Transformer Ratio}
	$Z_{\text{primary}} = Z_{\text{secondary}} \cdot \frac{25}{37500} = 1500\,\Omega$
	
	\medskip
	The Opamp provides a secondary load resistance of $37.5\,\Omega$, which is the input impedance of the preamp. The primary load resistance is $1500\,\Omega$, which is the output impedance of the opamp. The transformer ratio is 25:1, which means that the voltage at the primary side is 25 times the voltage at the secondary side.
