PRISM Technology

Hybrid Analog-Digital Architecture

The PRISM recording console features a revolutionary hybrid architecture that combines the warmth of analog circuitry with the precision and flexibility of digital processing. This design gives engineers the best of both worlds: the musical character of classic analog gear with the recallability and advanced features of modern digital systems.

Modular DSP Platform

At the heart of PRISM is a powerful, scalable DSP platform that allows for custom signal processing chains. Users can load different modules for EQ, compression, reverb, and other effects, creating a personalized mixing environment that adapts to their workflow.

Touch-Interface Workflow

PRISM incorporates a high-resolution touchscreen interface that provides intuitive control over all parameters. The interface is designed for both studio and live performance environments, with customizable layouts and presets.

Network Integration

With built-in Ethernet connectivity, PRISM can integrate seamlessly into modern studio networks. It supports AES67, Dante, and other audio-over-IP protocols for flexible routing and multi-device synchronization.

Sustainable Design

PRISM is designed with environmental considerations in mind. The power-efficient architecture reduces energy consumption, and the modular design allows for easy upgrades and repairs, extending the product lifecycle.
