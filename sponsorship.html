PRISM Sponsorship Opportunities

Why Sponsor PRISM?

PRISM represents the future of audio technology, and we're seeking visionary partners to help bring this innovation to musicians and audio professionals worldwide.

By sponsoring PRISM, your organization will:

  • Gain exposure to a passionate community of recording artists, producers, and engineers
  • Align your brand with cutting-edge audio innovation
  • Support the development of tools that empower musical creativity
  • Receive exclusive access to PRISM technology and events
