About PRISM

Our Story

PRISM was born from a passion for creating the ultimate recording experience. Founded by audio engineers and musicians who were frustrated with the limitations of existing consoles, we set out to design a product that truly meets the needs of modern music creators.

Our journey began in 2020 with a simple question: "What if we could combine the best aspects of analog and digital technology in a single, intuitive interface?" After years of research, development, and countless prototypes, PRISM is the answer to that question.

Our Mission

At KV DESIGNS, our mission is to empower musicians, producers, and audio engineers with tools that inspire creativity and eliminate technical barriers. We believe that great technology should be invisible - allowing artists to focus entirely on their music.

We strive to achieve this through:

  • Innovative hybrid analog-digital design
  • Intuitive user interfaces
  • Exceptional build quality and reliability
  • Continuous improvement through user feedback
